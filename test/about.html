Our Story

Flourette Corner began as a humble passion project in a small family kitchen. Our name represents the delicate blooming of flavors we strive to achieve in every bite.

We believe a cookie is more than just a dessert; it's a moment of refined indulgence. Every cookie is handcrafted with precision and passion in Metro Manila.

Crafting the most indulgent, artisanal cookies in Metro Manila. Every bite is a celebration of flavor and texture.
